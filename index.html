Operating Systems 4
EIOPS4A Course Hub

This course introduces students to High-Performance Computing (HPC), providing hands-on training to master enterprise multi-core architectures, commodity cluster provisioning, Slurm workload scheduling, OpenMP shared-memory pragmas, MPI distributed-memory message passing, and parallel storage systems.

Course Curriculum & Learning Modules

Select a week to access full student notes and step-by-step cluster lab guides.

12 Weeks Updated 2026
WEEK 01 Ch. 1 & 2

Introduction to HPC & Architecture

Supercomputing history, Flynn's taxonomy (SISD, SIMD, MISD, MIMD), Flynn's limits, South African CHPC Lengau & NICIS context.

Student Notes Lab 1 Guide
WEEK 02 Ch. 3

Commodity Clusters & System Design

Beowulf cluster architecture, commodity off-the-shelf components, head nodes, compute nodes, and private cluster networks.

Student Notes Theory Only
WEEK 03 Ch. 4 & 5

Node Provisioning Strategies

General provisioning concepts, Infrastructure as Code (Vagrant & VirtualBox), bare-metal stateless booting (Warewulf 4), and trade-off selection.

Student Notes Lab 3 Guide
WEEK 04 Ch. 6 & 7

Resource Management with Slurm

Slurm workload architecture (slurmctld, slurmd), fair-share priority math, cgroups v2 memory enforcement, sbatch job scripts.

Student Notes Lab 4 Guide
WEEK 05 Ch. 7

Shared-Memory Programming with OpenMP

Fork-Join thread lifecycle, data scoping (shared, private, reduction), race condition fixes, static/dynamic loop scheduling.

Student Notes Lab 3 Guide
WEEK 06 Ch. 8

Distributed-Memory Programming with MPI

SPMD model, communicators, ranks, non-blocking MPI_Isend/MPI_Irecv, collective operations, mpi4py, and Hybrid MPI+OpenMP.

Student Notes Lab 4 Guide
WEEK 07 Ch. 9 & 10

Parallel Algorithms & Numerical Libraries

Work/Span analysis, domain decomposition, Foster's methodology, parallel matrix algorithms, BLAS/LAPACK, ScaLAPACK, PETSc.

Student Notes Lab 5 Guide
WEEK 08 Ch. 4 & 13

Benchmarking & Performance Monitoring

High-Performance Linpack (HPL), STREAM memory bandwidth kernels, Roofline Model equations, Prometheus & Grafana telemetry.

Student Notes Lab 6 Guide
WEEK 09 Ch. 14

Debugging Parallel Applications

Concurrency failures, GDB multi-process debugging workflows, Valgrind Memcheck/Helgrind suite, GCC sanitizers (-fsanitize=address).

Student Notes Theory Only
WEEK 10 Ch. 15 & 16

Accelerator & GPU Architecture

Heterogeneous Host-Device PCIe models, CUDA 3-tier thread grid hierarchy, HBM3 memory bandwidth, OpenACC directives.

Student Notes Theory Only
WEEK 11 Ch. 17 & 18

High-Performance Parallel File Systems

Lustre & BeeGFS architecture, Metadata Servers (MDS/MDT) vs Object Storage Servers (OSS/OST), file striping (lfs setstripe), MPI-IO.

Student Notes Lab 7 Guide
WEEK 12 · CAPSTONE HPC Automation

Capstone Project — HPC Cluster Synthesis & Live Demo

Synthesis of Labs 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, and Chapter 8 MPI: Pre-configured 5-node baseline, compiling Lab 4 scientific libraries (OpenBLAS, FFTW3, HDF5, ScaLAPACK, PETSc) with Lmod, centralized Python with mpi4python, HPL benchmarking, and live on-the-fly algorithm execution in pairs.
