Week 4 Presentation Deck: Resource Management & Slurm Scheduling

SMP, NUMA Architectures, Slurm Daemons & Cgroups Enforcements

Slide 1: Single-Node Multiprocessing (SMP & NUMA)

  • Symmetric Multiprocessing (SMP): Multiple identical sockets sharing main RAM under one kernel.
  • NUMA Nodes: Split memory controllers per CPU socket. Local RAM access (~60 ns) vs Remote RAM access (~140 ns).
  • False Sharing: Cache line bouncing across cores caused by independent variables residing on the same 64-byte line. Solution: C++17 `alignas(64)`.

Slide 2: Slurm Workload Manager Architecture

  • slurmctld: Central controller managing queues, node states, and job allocations.
  • slurmd: Compute node daemon executing tasks and enforcing Cgroup CPU/RAM limits.
  • slurmdbd: Database daemon tracking historical accounting and fair-share usage in MySQL.

Slide 3: Slurm Job Submission & Queuing

  • Client Commands: `sbatch` (batch submission), `srun` (parallel step launch), `squeue` (queue inspection), `scancel` (job cancellation), `sinfo` (node state summary).
  • Scheduling Policies: First-In First-Out (FIFO) with Backfill Scheduling and Fair-Share Priority Scoring.
